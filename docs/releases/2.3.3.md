# LLM Monitor 2.3.3

## English

Quota health now pairs effective 5-hour quota with 5-hour remaining time, and raw weekly quota with weekly remaining time. Overall health takes the worse tier. This fixes yellow indicators caused by dividing a weekly-constrained effective 5-hour remainder by weekly time. Progress bars, menu-bar metrics, and edge-dock rings use the corrected scales. Weekly-only quotas use raw weekly percentages; missing reset times fall back to fixed thresholds.

## 简体中文

修复额度健康色的时间尺度：有效 5h 余量配 5h 时间，原始周余量配周时间，综合健康取较差档。周额度构成瓶颈时，不再把折算后的 5h 余量除以周时间而误判黄色。进度条、状态栏指标及边缘窗圆环同步使用正确尺度；仅周窗口直接使用原始周百分比，缺少重置时间时按固定阈值判色。
