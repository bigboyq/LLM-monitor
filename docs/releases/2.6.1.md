# LLM Monitor 2.6.1

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

### Changed

- **Speed now measures decoding throughput: the denominator excludes TTFT.** With every major harness reporting per-call TTFT, all three speed surfaces (quota-window rate, prompt-level P50 in the harness list, daily P50 in the seven-day history) switch from Σtokens ÷ Σcall-duration to Σtokens ÷ Σ(duration − TTFT). Calls without TTFT data count their full duration (user decision); calls with TTFT ≥ duration are treated as ledger anomalies and excluded entirely; a prompt joins the distribution only when every call has a positive decode duration. Tooltips updated accordingly. No cache rebuild needed — this is a compute-side change over the already-stored `ttftSeconds`.

## 简体中文

### 变更

- **速度口径升级为解码吞吐：分母扣除 TTFT。** 主要 harness 均已具备逐调用 TTFT，三处速度（额度窗口速度、Harness 列表 Prompt P50、7 天历史每日 P50）从 Σtokens ÷ Σ调用时长 改为 Σtokens ÷ Σ(时长 − TTFT)。无 TTFT 数据的调用按时长全额计入（用户裁定）；`ttft ≥ duration` 的异常调用整条剔除；prompt 内任一调用解码时长非正则整个 prompt 不进分布。三处 tooltip 口径同步改写。纯计算侧变更（`ttftSeconds` 已在样本中），无需重建缓存。
