# LLM Monitor 2.6.4

## English

### Fixed

- Normalize ChatGPT quota windows by their reported duration. A weekly-only Pro quota in `primary_window` now contributes to weekly usage and the status bar's weekly arc instead of 5h.
- Preserve the distinction between an absent window and an exhausted window: weekly-only plans have no synthetic 5h quota; an exhausted weekly quota still reduces dual-window effective availability to zero.

## 简体中文

### 修复

- ChatGPT 额度按接口返回时长归一化：Pro 将唯一周窗口放在 `primary_window` 时，用量统计与状态栏正确归入周额度。
- 区分窗口缺失与额度耗尽：仅周套餐不生成虚假的 5h 额度；双窗口的周额度耗尽时，有效可用额度仍为零。
