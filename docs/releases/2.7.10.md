# LLM Monitor 2.7.10

## English

### Fixed

- **The menu bar iconDuo left arc (5h quota) now shows the effective quota `min(5h remaining, weekly remaining × N)`**, the same caliber as the center wedge, the three bottom dots and the edge-dock outer ring. N is dispatched per provider (`weeklyEquivalentMultiplier`: Antigravity Gemini 4 / Claude and GPT = 4 / 2, MiniMax general / video = 10 / 7, GLM Coding Plan = 5, Codex ChatGPT = 6); the right arc still shows the raw weekly remainder, and DeepSeek's single-window balance is unaffected. When the weekly quota is tighter, the left arc shrinks with it instead of overestimating the available 5h quota. Card progress bars keep their physical-window readings by design.

## 简体中文

### 修复

- **菜单栏 iconDuo 左弧（5h 额度）改为有效额度 `min(5h 剩余, 周剩余 × 周等效倍率 N)`**，与中心扇形 / 底部三点 / 边缘窗外环同口径。N 按 provider 分派（`weeklyEquivalentMultiplier`：Antigravity Gemini 4 / Claude and GPT = 4 / 2，MiniMax general / video = 10 / 7，GLM Coding Plan = 5，Codex ChatGPT = 6）；右弧仍显示原始周剩余，DeepSeek 单窗余额型不受影响。周额度更紧时左弧随之收缩，不再高估可用 5h 额度；卡片进度条维持物理窗口读数不变（有意设计）。
